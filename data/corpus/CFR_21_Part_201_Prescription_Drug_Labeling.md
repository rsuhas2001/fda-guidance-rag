# DOC_ID: CFR-201-LABELING
# TITLE: 21 CFR Part 201 — Drug Labeling & Physician Labeling Rule (PLR) Requirements
# SOURCE: U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Code of Federal Regulations Title 21 Part 201

## Section 1: General Drug Labeling Requirements (21 CFR 201.1 - 201.50)
Under 21 CFR Part 201, no prescription or biological drug product may be introduced into interstate commerce unless its immediate container label and outer carton bear:
- **Established Name and Proprietary Name**: The established (nonproprietary/generic) name must accompany the proprietary name in type size at least half as large as the proprietary name (21 CFR 201.10(g)(2)).
- **National Drug Code (NDC)**: A unique 10-digit or 11-digit three-segment identifier (Labeler Code, Product Code, and Package Code) under 21 CFR 201.2 and 21 CFR Part 207.
- **Lot Number and Expiration Date**: The control/lot number enabling complete manufacturing batch history traceability (21 CFR 201.18) and a stability-supported expiration date (21 CFR 201.17).
- **Bar Code Requirement (21 CFR 201.25)**: All human prescription drug products and biological products used in hospitals must bear a linear barcode encoding the NDC number at the minimum to prevent bedside medication administration errors.

## Section 2: Physician Labeling Rule (PLR) Format (21 CFR 201.56 & 201.57)
Prescription drug Prescribing Information (PI) must follow the standardized Physician Labeling Rule (PLR) structure:
1. **Highlights of Prescribing Information (Half-Page Summary)**: Must fit within one-half page (excluding Boxed Warning) and include Product Names, Date of Initial U.S. Approval, Boxed Warning (if applicable), Recent Major Changes (within the past 12 months), Indications and Usage, Dosage and Administration, Dosage Forms and Strengths, Contraindications, Warnings and Precautions, Adverse Reactions, and Drug Interactions.
2. **Full Prescribing Information (FPI) Table of Contents**: Ordered sequentially from Section 1 (*Indications and Usage*) through Section 17 (*Patient Counseling Information*).
3. **Section 16 (How Supplied/Storage and Handling)**: Must specify the exact container closure description (e.g., single-dose vs. multiple-dose glass vial, prefilled syringe volume, stopper elastomer latex-free status), NDC numbers per package configuration, and validated storage temperature excursion limits (e.g., 2°C to 8°C refrigerated; protect from light; do not freeze).
