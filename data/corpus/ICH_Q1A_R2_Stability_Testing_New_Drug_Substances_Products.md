# DOC_ID: ICH-Q1A-STABILITY
# TITLE: FDA / ICH Q1A(R2) Guidance for Industry: Stability Testing of New Drug Substances and Products
# SOURCE: U.S. Food and Drug Administration / ICH Q1A(R2), November 2003

## Section 1: Standard Storage Conditions and Minimum Time Period at Submission
Under ICH Q1A(R2), stability studies for a new drug product must be conducted in the **exact container closure system** proposed for marketing (including primary vial/syringe, stopper, and secondary packaging):
1. **General Case (Room Temperature Storage)**:
   - **Long-Term**: `25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH` (or `30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH`) for a minimum of **12 months** at submission.
   - **Intermediate**: `30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH` for **6 months** (required if "significant change" occurs at accelerated conditions).
   - **Accelerated**: `40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH` for a minimum of **6 months**.
2. **Drug Products Intended for Storage in a Refrigerator (e.g., Biologics & Parenterals)**:
   - **Long-Term**: `5°C ± 3°C` for a minimum of **12 months**.
   - **Accelerated**: `25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH` for a minimum of **6 months**.
3. **Drug Products Intended for Storage in a Freezer**:
   - **Long-Term**: `-20°C ± 5°C` for a minimum of **12 months**.

## Section 2: Semi-Permeable Containers and Moisture Loss (Low Relative Humidity Testing)
Aqueous-based drug products packaged in **semi-permeable containers** (such as low-density polyethylene [LDPE] blow-fill-seal ampules, ophthalmic dropper bottles, or flexible IV bags) must be evaluated for **water loss** in addition to physical, chemical, biological, and microbiological stability:
- Long-term testing is conducted at `25°C ± 2°C / 40% RH ± 5% RH` and accelerated testing at `40°C ± 2°C / no more than (NMT) 25% RH`.
- A **5% loss in water** from its initial value is considered a **significant change** for a product packaged in a semi-permeable container after an equivalent of 3 months' storage at `40°C / NMT 25% RH`.

## Section 3: Definition of "Significant Change" for Drug Products
Under ICH Q1A(R2), a **significant change** at accelerated storage is defined as any of the following:
- A **5% change in assay** from its initial value, or failure to meet acceptance criteria for potency when using biological or immunological procedures;
- Any **degradation product exceeding its acceptance criterion**;
- Failure to meet acceptance criteria for **appearance, physical attributes, and functionality tests** (e.g., color, phase separation, resuspendability, caking, hardness, dose delivery per actuation, or syringe glide force); or
- Failure to meet the acceptance criterion for **pH** or **dissolution** for 12 dosage units.
