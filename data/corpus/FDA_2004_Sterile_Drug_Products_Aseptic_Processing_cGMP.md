# DOC_ID: FDA-ASEPTIC-2004
# TITLE: FDA Guidance for Industry: Sterile Drug Products Produced by Aseptic Processing — Current Good Manufacturing Practice
# SOURCE: U.S. Food and Drug Administration (CDER/CBER/ORA), September 2004

## Section 1: Cleanroom Classification and Environmental Monitoring Limits
FDA aseptic processing guidance establishes microbiological and airborne particulate standards across cleanroom classifications during dynamic operations (measured at particles `>= 0.5 µm` per cubic meter):
- **ISO Class 5 (Class 100 / Critical Area)**: Maximum **3,520 particles/m³** (`>= 0.5 µm`), active air action limit of **1 CFU/m³**, settling plates (90 mm, 4 hours) action limit of **1 CFU**, and glove fingertip sampling limit of **< 1 CFU (no growth)**. All open sterile containers, elastomeric stoppers, and filling needles must remain in ISO Class 5 unidirectional (laminar) airflow (`0.45 m/s ± 20%`, or 90 feet/min).
- **ISO Class 7 (Class 10,000 / Supporting Clean Area)**: Maximum **352,000 particles/m³** (`>= 0.5 µm`), active air action limit of **10 CFU/m³**, and settling plate limit of **5 CFU/4 hours**.
- **ISO Class 8 (Class 100,000)**: Maximum **3,520,000 particles/m³** (`>= 0.5 µm`) and active air action limit of **100 CFU/m³**.

## Section 2: Process Simulation (Media Fills) Acceptance Criteria
To qualify an aseptic filling and stoppering line, manufacturers must perform **media fills** (process simulations using sterile Soybean-Casein Digest Medium / Tryptic Soy Broth [TSB]) semi-annually (twice per year) per shift and filling line:
- **Minimum Media Fill Run Size**: At least **5,000 to 10,000 units** (or the full commercial batch size if smaller than 5,000 units), incubating filled vials at `20°C–25°C` for 7 days followed by `30°C–35°C` for 7 days (14 days total).
- **Contamination Acceptance Criteria**:
  - For runs of **< 5,000 units**: **Zero contaminated units** permitted (any positive unit requires revalidation).
  - For runs of **5,000 to 10,000 units**: **One (1) contaminated unit** triggers an investigation and consideration of a repeat media fill; **two (2) contaminated units** are grounds for immediate revalidation.
  - For runs of **> 10,000 units**: **One (1) contaminated unit** triggers an investigation; **two (2) contaminated units** are grounds for revalidation.

## Section 3: Sterilizing Filtration Validation (0.22 µm Filters)
A sterilizing-grade membrane filter (nominally rated at **0.22 µm or smaller**) must be validated using a microbiological challenge test with ***Brevundimonas diminuta*** (ATCC 19146) at a minimum concentration of **10^7 organisms per cm²** of effective filtration area, yielding a completely sterile filtrate. Every sterilizing filter must undergo non-destructive integrity testing (such as **Bubble Point**, **Diffusive Flow**, or **Pressure Hold** testing) both **pre-use (post-sterilization)** and **post-use** before batch release.
