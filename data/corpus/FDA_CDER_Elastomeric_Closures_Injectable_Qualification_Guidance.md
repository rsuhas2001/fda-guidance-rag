# DOC_ID: FDA-ELASTOMERS-CMC
# TITLE: FDA CDER CMC Review Standards: Qualification of Elastomeric Closures for Injectable Drug Products (21 CFR 211.94 & 211.84)
# SOURCE: U.S. Food and Drug Administration (CDER) Manual of Policies and Procedures (MAPP) & Public CMC Guidance (Public Domain, 17 U.S.C. 105)

## Section 1: Classification of Coated vs. Uncoated Elastomeric Closures
In FDA Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) submissions (21 CFR 314.50), elastomeric vial stoppers and syringe plungers are categorized by barrier design:
- **Type I (Uncoated Elastomeric Closures)**: Standard halobutyl (bromobutyl or chlorobutyl) or polyisoprene rubber stoppers where the elastomer directly contacts the injectable formulation.
- **Type II (Barrier-Coated / Laminated Elastomeric Closures)**: Elastomeric closures laminated or coated with an inert fluoropolymer barrier film (such as PTFE, ETFE, or cross-linked silicone) to minimize drug adsorption, silicone oil interaction, and extractable metal/oligomer migration into biologics and oncology parenterals.

## Section 2: Biological Reactivity and Physicochemical Extract Limits
All elastomeric components in direct contact with parenteral solutions must demonstrate biological safety and chemical inertness:
1. **Biological Reactivity**: In vitro cytotoxicity testing using **L-929 mammalian fibroblast cells** (elution test requiring **Grade 0** reactivity and agar diffusion/direct contact requiring **Grade <= 2**) and in vivo systemic/intracutaneous reactivity testing when applicable.
2. **Aqueous Autoclave Extract Limits (121°C for 30 Minutes, 100 cm² per 200 mL Water)**:
   - **Turbidity**: `<= 6 NTU` for both Type I and Type II closures.
   - **Acidity or Alkalinity**: Not more than `0.3 mL of 0.01 N NaOH` or `0.8 mL of 0.01 N HCl` per 20 mL extract.
   - **UV Absorbance (220–360 nm)**: `<= 0.2 AU` for Type I closures.
   - **Reducing Substances**: Titration difference `<= 3.0 mL` of 0.01 N sodium thiosulfate.
   - **Extractable Zinc**: `<= 5 ppm (5 µg/mL)` in aqueous extract for zinc-cured halobutyl rubber.

## Section 3: Mechanical Functionality Qualification for Vial Stoppers
FDA CMC review requires three mechanical performance tests for punctured elastomeric vial stoppers:
- **Penetrability Force**: Using a lubricated **21-gauge hypodermic needle** (`0.8 mm` outer diameter) at `200 mm/min`, puncture force across 10 stoppers must **not exceed 10 N (1.0 kgf)**.
- **Fragmentation (Coring Resistance)**: Across **48 total punctures** (12 vials punctured 4 times each with a 21-gauge needle), visible elastomer fragments retained on a 50-µm filter must be **<= 5 fragments**.
- **Self-Sealing Capacity (Multi-Dose Vials)**: After 10 punctures with a 21-gauge needle and immersion in `0.1% methylene blue` under `27 kPa` vacuum for 10 minutes followed by 30 minutes at ambient pressure, **0 out of 10 vials** may exhibit dye ingress.
